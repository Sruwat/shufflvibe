The short version of how we keep SHUFFL useful, respectful, and worth coming back to.

On this pageCommunityVenuesSafety

Community first

SHUFFL is built for real people making real plans. Be honest in your profile, respectful in rooms, and clear when a plan is no longer right for you.

Venue information

We work to keep venue details useful and current, but hours, availability, entry rules, and events can change. Always check with the venue before you travel.

Look out for each other

Meet in public places, share your plans with someone you trust, and use your own judgement. SHUFFL is a planning tool, not a substitute for personal safety or local guidance.
