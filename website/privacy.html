What we collect

We collect the information you choose to give us, such as your profile details, vibe answers, preferences, rooms joined, and venue interactions. We also collect basic technical information needed to keep the app secure and working.

How we use it

We use this information to create your daily vibe, match you with venues and rooms, improve recommendations, communicate with you, and understand what parts of SHUFFL are useful.

Your choices

You can review or update your profile, change preferences, and ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information by contacting hello@shuffl.app.
