SH:FFL · F, matched to the letters

The bounding box was already the right height — the perceived height was not, because a rotated card presents a corner at its extremes and a corner reads as nothing. Flat letters read to their full cap height; angled and round forms have to overshoot to look equal, which is the same reason a typographer draws an O taller than an H. The mark now runs y26–108 against the letters' y30–104 — three units proud top and bottom — and the gaps either side are cut to 34.

THE LOCKUP
Cards up from 44×64 to 47×68, circle from r29 to r33, and the whole mark re-centred on the letters' optical middle. It now reads as the same size as the characters either side of it rather than as something tucked between them.
THE LOCKUP ON LIGHT
Cyan drops to near-black on paper-white, as it did on the first sheet. Pink stays pink on both grounds, which is what makes it the brand colour rather than the letters.
