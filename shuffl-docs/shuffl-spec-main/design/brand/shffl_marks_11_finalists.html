SH:FFL · the two that are left

01 is the colour-forward one: the cards carry the brand pair and the disc stays white. 10a is the inverse: the cards go neutral and all the colour moves into the letters and the lines, crossed so each card appears to be moving into the other one’s colour. Both on the eight with the open centre.

01 · COLOURED CARDS
Louder and more immediate. Pink and cyan arrive before anything else and the white disc sits between them as the still point. The cost is that four hues and three values is a lot of mark to manage, and the disc competes with the cards for brightness.
10A · WHITE CARDS, CROSSED LINES
Quieter and more deliberate. The colour is in the type, where the name is, and the crossed lines are the only place the two colours meet — which reads as one event rather than two objects. Holds its contrast further down in size than 01.

On the light ground

01 ON LIGHT
Cyan collapses toward black on paper-white and the disc needs a dark ring added or it dissolves into the page. Two changes to the artwork, every time.
10A ON LIGHT
The cards invert and nothing else moves. The letters and the crossed lines keep their colours because both sit on the card rather than on the page. One change, and it is a swap.
