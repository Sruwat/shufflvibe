SH:FFL · type treatments, and the crowd

All three typefaces below are already in the product — Bebas Neue names things, Archivo is the display face, IBM Plex Mono writes every door time. Nothing new is being introduced. And the crowd: four dots in a symmetric diamond is a die face, which is why it reads as a ball. Everything after it breaks the symmetry one way or another.

Three type treatments on option 1

BEBAS NEUE · CROSS-COLOURED
The house display face, with each card carrying the other card's colour. Ties the two halves together and keeps the pink/cyan pair doing work inside the mark rather than only as card fills.
ARCHIVO 800 · WHITE
Wider, heavier, and modern. White on both reads as the most confident of the three and is the only one that stays legible if the cards ever go to a single colour.
IBM PLEX MONO · INK
The face the app writes 21:00 in. Mono ties the wordmark to the colon, which is the one piece of the name doing conceptual work — the clock separator every stop is written with.

Seven crowds, same circle

THE ONE WE HAVE
Four dots in a symmetric diamond. That is a die face, and the eye knows it - which is why it reads as a ball rather than a room.
A · SCATTER
Eight, irregular, slightly different sizes. Nothing lines up, which is what makes it people. Regularity is the whole problem with the original.
B · CLUSTERED
Same count, but bunched left with space on the right. Real crowds clump and leave gaps; an even spread is the thing that never happens in a room.
C · NEAR AND FAR
Five dots with a wide size range. Size reads as distance, so the circle gains depth and stops being a flat pattern. Fewest parts of any option here.
D · FACING IN
Six around the edge with the middle empty. This is the app's own room screen - members' icons ringing the blend style. The most product-true of the six, and the most regular, which is the tension.
E · ONE OF THEM IS YOU
The scatter, with one dot in the live colour - you, in the room. Says what the product is in one dot, and needs two colours to say it.
F · OVERLAPPING
Four large discs that overlap. Reads as people standing close rather than dots on a plane, and holds its shape when everything shrinks because the forms are big.
