SH:FFL · eight with an open centre, and the crossed lines

Crowd B throughout — eight circles, all one radius, evenly spaced on the edge with nothing in the middle. It is the arrangement the app already draws on its own room screen, members ringing the blend, so the logo and the product now make the same shape.

01, 03 and 10, on the eight

01 · WHITE DISC
White disc, ink crowd. The open centre helps this one most — the old solid middle made the disc read as a filled ball, and the hole is what turns it into a room seen from above.
03 · DARK DISC, WHITE CROWD
Dark disc, white crowd. Still the only combination that is identical on both grounds, and still the most legible at badge size.
10 · WHITE CARDS, CROSSED
The new one. Cards go white, SH is pink and FFL is cyan, and each set of lines takes the other card's colour — cyan beside the pink letters, pink beside the cyan.

Working out the crossed lines on 10

The crossing is worth testing against its alternatives, because the lines are the only part of the mark that carries motion and whichever colour they take is the colour the eye reads as moving.

10a · CROSSED · SH PINK
As asked. Pink letters with cyan lines, cyan letters with pink lines. The crossing makes each card look like it is moving into the other one's colour, which is the right story for two cards swiping apart around a shared room.
10b · MATCHED · FOR COMPARISON
Lines matching their own card. Reads as two separate objects rather than one event — which is exactly what the crossing fixes. Here only to show the difference.
10c · CROSSED · SH CYAN
The same crossing with the two letter colours swapped. Pink on the right is heavier, and since FFL is the longer word it makes the mark lean right. 10a is better balanced.
10d · CROSSED · WHITE DISC
Same crossing, white disc with an ink crowd. Three white shapes in a row — the disc stops being distinct from the cards and the mark flattens.
10e · CROSSED · ON PINK CARDS
The crossing applied to 01's colours instead. It barely registers, because a cyan line beside a pink card reads as part of the cyan card's trail. The device only works when the cards are neutral.
10f · TRUE MONO · ONE INK
Keep this one too. 10 used to be the single-ink version, and adding colour to it means the system still needs a no-colour mark for embroidery, a stamp or a newspaper.

On the light ground

01 ON LIGHT
Cyan still collapses on paper-white. The disc needs the dark ring here or it dissolves.
03 ON LIGHT
Untouched by the flip. Nothing is redrawn for the inverse.
10 ON LIGHT
The cards invert to black and everything else holds — the letters and the crossed lines keep their colours, because both sit on the card rather than on the page.
